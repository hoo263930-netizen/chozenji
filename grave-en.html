Graves at Chozenji Temple

Chozenji Temple offers two types of graves: an Eitai-kuyo memorial grave and private graves. These options are intended for people who are concerned about the future care and succession of a family grave, or who are considering a new place for burial and memorial services.

Please feel free to consult us if there is no one to inherit the grave, if you do not wish to leave the burden of grave care to your family, or if you would like to have a grave for yourself or your family.

Through a visit and consultation, we will explain the fees, the process before burial, and how memorial services will be conducted in the future.

Two Types of Graves

Eitai-kuyo Memorial Grave

This grave is intended for people who do not have someone to inherit a family grave, or who are concerned about the future management of a grave. The remains are placed in the memorial grave and cared for by Chozenji Temple.

This option may be suitable for those who do not wish to place the burden of grave care on their children or relatives, those looking for a place for burial, or those who wish to prepare during their lifetime.

Fee
¥200,000
Details about burial and memorial services will be explained during consultation.

Private Grave

This option is for people who would like to have a grave for themselves or their family. It is suitable for those who wish to keep a more traditional form of grave, where family members can visit and offer prayers.

The number of available plots is limited. If you are interested, we will show you the actual location and explain the conditions of use and management.

Fee
¥500,000
Private grave plots are limited. Please contact us for availability.

From Consultation to Burial

Contact Us

Please contact us through the inquiry form. You do not need to decide in advance whether you are interested in an Eitai-kuyo memorial grave or a private grave.

Consultation and Visit

We will show you the actual grave area and ask about your family situation and wishes.

Confirm Details and Fees

We will explain the burial process, fees, management details, and necessary preparations.

Application

After confirming the details, you may proceed with the application.

Burial and Memorial Service

We will arrange the date and conduct the burial and memorial service.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have not decided between an Eitai-kuyo memorial grave and a private grave.

You are welcome to consult us even before deciding. We will explain the differences while listening to your family situation and concerns about future grave care.

Can I visit the grave before deciding?

Yes. Please contact us in advance, and we will arrange a time to show you the grave area.

Can I consult during my lifetime?

Yes. You may consult us in preparation for your own future or for your family.

How much does it cost?

The Eitai-kuyo memorial grave is ¥200,000, and a private grave is ¥500,000. Private grave plots are limited, so please contact us for availability.
