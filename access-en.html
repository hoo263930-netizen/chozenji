Access

Address, map, and parking information for visitors to Chozenji Temple.

LOCATION

Location

Temple Name
Chozenji Temple
Address
581 Koaraji, Makino-cho,
Takashima City, Shiga 520-1803, Japan
Parking
There are two parking areas. Parking Area 1 is located up the road beside the temple and can accommodate about 10 cars. Parking Area 2 is located along the road and can accommodate about 5 cars. Visitors may use either parking area.
Inquiries
Please contact us in advance for memorial services, zazen experiences, memorial graves, temple visits, or other inquiries.

When using Google Maps or a car navigation system, please search by the address or by “Chozenji Temple.”

If you are visiting for an event, memorial service, zazen experience, or other appointment, the guidance may vary depending on the date, time, and number of visitors. Please contact us in advance when necessary.

PARKING

Parking Information

If you are coming by car, please refer to the parking map below. There are two parking areas, and visitors may use either one.

Parking Area 1 is located up the road beside the temple and can accommodate about 10 cars. Parking Area 2 is located along the road and can accommodate about 5 cars.

Some areas do not have clearly marked parking lines. If you are coming with several cars or are unsure where to park, please contact us in advance.
