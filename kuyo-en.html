ABOUT

What Chozenji Can Offer

Chozenji accepts inquiries about Buddhist memorial services, offerings, and prayers, including from people who are not temple members. If you do not already have a connection with a temple, or if you are not sure what kind of service you need, please feel free to contact us.

We will ask about your family situation, your relationship with the deceased, and your preferred place and date, then guide you through the possible options.

CONSULTATION

Types of Consultation

Annual memorial services

You can ask about family memorial services such as the first, third, seventh, and other anniversary services.

Prayers for the deceased

We accept inquiries from those who wish to honor and remember a loved one through sutra chanting and Buddhist prayer.

Sutra chanting at graves or home altars

Please contact us about prayers in front of a grave or family Buddhist altar, including sutra chanting related to interment.

Buddhist prayers and blessings

Please contact us about prayers for family safety, physical well-being, personal wishes, and other occasions.

For non-members

Even if you do not have an existing relationship with Chozenji, we can listen to your situation and explain possible options.

If you are not sure what to request

You may contact us even if you are not sure what kind of memorial service or prayer is appropriate.

MENU

Main Services

Annual memorial services

Memorial services can be held in the main hall of Chozenji, at your home, at a grave, or in another appropriate place. The date and location will be arranged through consultation.

Memorial prayers for ancestors and the deceased

You can ask about prayers and offerings to remember ancestors or a deceased loved one. Even if it is not a specific annual memorial service, we can listen to your situation and guide you.

Prayers related to interment

Please contact us about sutra chanting for interment, or prayers related to memorial graves and individual graves.

Buddhist prayers and blessings

Please contact us about prayers for family safety, physical well-being, personal wishes, and other important moments in daily life.

OFFERING

Offerings and Fees

Suggested offerings

Buddhist offerings are generally given according to one’s circumstances and intention. For first-time visitors, the following amounts are provided as a general guide.

Memorial services / prayers From 10,000 yen
Sutra chanting at a grave From 5,000 yen

The actual amount may vary depending on the content, location, preparation, and other conditions. Details will be explained after we confirm your situation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I request a service even if I am not a temple member?

Yes. Chozenji accepts inquiries about memorial services, offerings, and prayers from people who are not temple members.

Can I ask even if I am not sure what kind of service I need?

Yes. We will listen to your situation and explain what may be appropriate.

Can the priest come to my home or a grave?

Depending on the content and location, this may be possible. Please contact us first.

How much does it cost?

Offerings are generally given according to one’s circumstances and intention. As a general guide, memorial services and prayers start from 10,000 yen, and sutra chanting at a grave starts from 5,000 yen. The details may vary depending on the content, so we will explain them after consultation.

Can I ask for an urgent date?

Depending on the temple schedule, it may be possible. Please contact us first.
