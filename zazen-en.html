About this experience

A calm introduction to zazen for first-time visitors

This is not a strict training session. It is a private, small-group experience for people who would like to sit quietly in a temple, learn the basic posture and breathing, and spend time away from daily noise.

Chozenji Temple is located in Makino, Takashima City, Shiga Prefecture, near the well-known Metasequoia Avenue. The experience is suitable for travelers, couples, small groups, and visitors interested in Japanese temple culture.

Simple English guidance is available. Please note that this is not a professional interpretation service, but the basic flow and meaning of zazen can be explained in simple English.

About the priest

Guided by a priest trained at Eiheiji Temple

The priest of Chozenji Temple trained at Eiheiji Temple, one of the head temples of Soto Zen Buddhism, for four and a half years.

In this experience, the guidance is kept simple and accessible. You do not need previous meditation experience, knowledge of Buddhism, or the ability to sit cross-legged.

Chair seating can be arranged for those who have difficulty sitting on the floor.

Program

Program flow

The program usually takes about 90 minutes. The exact flow may be adjusted depending on the number of participants and the condition of the day.

0:00

Arrival and welcome

Please come to Chozenji Temple at the confirmed time. We will guide you into the temple hall and explain the basic flow.

0:10

Introduction to posture and breathing

You will learn how to sit, how to place your hands, and how to breathe during zazen. Chair seating is also possible.

0:25

First zazen sitting

We begin with a short sitting period so that beginners can experience zazen without strain.

0:45

Short break

There will be a short break. You may ask simple questions before the second sitting.

0:55

Second zazen sitting

We sit quietly again, with attention to posture, breathing, and the atmosphere of the temple.

1:15

Temple introduction and questions

After zazen, we can briefly introduce the temple and answer questions about zazen, Buddhism, or temple culture.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this experience suitable for beginners?

Yes. This experience is designed for first-time visitors. Basic posture, breathing, and manners will be explained slowly.

Do I need to be Buddhist?

No. You do not need to be Buddhist. This is a quiet temple experience open to visitors who would like to learn about zazen respectfully.

Can I sit on a chair?

Yes. Chair seating can be arranged. Please tell us in advance when you send your booking request.

Can I take photos?

Photos may be allowed in some areas, but not everywhere. Please ask before taking photos inside the temple.

Can children join?

Children may join depending on age and group situation. Please contact us in advance.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change the number of participants. No-show without notice may affect future bookings.

Is this a professional English tour?

No. Simple English guidance is available, but this is not a professional interpretation service. The program is designed to be simple and easy to follow.
